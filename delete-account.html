Delete Your Account

PropsTrack: Film Prop Tracker · by Martin Peev

You can delete your PropsTrack account and personal data at any time, directly in the app or by email.

1

Delete in the app

Open PropsTrack, go to Profile, scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm with Delete Permanently. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

2

Or ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email propstrack@icloud.com from the address you signed up with and write "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

3

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details, your name, email address, profile photo, push notification token, your membership of every project, and the usage statistics linked to your account.

4

What is kept

Props, photos and notes you logged in a project are part of that production's shared records, so they stay with the project for the rest of the crew, no longer linked to your account. A project admin can delete them, or the whole project, at any time; you can also ask us by email to remove them. Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days.

Questions?

We're happy to help with anything about your data.
